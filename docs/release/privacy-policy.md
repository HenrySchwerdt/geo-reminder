# Worktime Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-10-29. Effective for Worktime 1.0.0 and later.*

This policy is written to match the in-app Privacy screen (Settings > Privacy). It is a template: fill in the bracketed fields, have it checked for your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR information duties), and host it at a public URL (for example GitHub Pages) before submitting to the App Store and Google Play.

## Short version
Worktime keeps your working hours on your phone. We have no server and no account system, so we **cannot see, collect or recover your data**. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

## What the app stores, and where
Worktime stores the following **only on your device** (in the app's private database and storage):
- the time entries you create or confirm (clock in, break, clock out, edits, deletions);
- the workplaces you create (name, the coordinates of the place you chose, radius) and your reminder rules;
- your settings and the app's own state needed to run reminders.

Nobody else, including us, can access this data. It is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and by your device's own security (passcode, device encryption, backups you have enabled). If your phone is included in an iCloud or Google device backup, the operating system may back up app data under your own account according to your phone settings; we do not receive it.

## How the app uses your location
If you allow it, Worktime uses your device's location **only** to notice when you arrive at or leave a workplace that you created, so it can ask you "Clock in?" or "Clock out?". To save battery, the operating system watches the area around your workplaces; only when you are close does the app request a few location readings, and it stops once arrival or departure is decided. On Android a notification is visible while this runs.

- The app **does not record your route** or location history. The only coordinates stored are those of the workplaces you add.
- Location is **never sent off your device**, to us or anyone else.
- Background ("Always" / "Allow all the time") access is optional. Without it the app still works with manual clock in/out and time-based reminders.
- You can change or withdraw location permission at any time in your phone's system settings.

## Notifications
Reminders are local notifications created on your device. No push-notification service or server is used. You can turn them off in system settings.

## Export, import and deletion
- **Export:** Settings > Your data lets you export all entries as CSV or a full JSON backup. The file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share it (for example Files, Mail or another app). From that point the receiving app's policy applies.
- **Import:** you can restore a JSON backup from a file on your device.
- **Delete:** Settings > Your data > Delete all data erases all entries, workplaces and settings from the app. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further to request from us.

## Sharing with third parties
None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or data-collecting SDKs. We do not sell or share personal data. (The Apple and Google app stores process information about downloads under their own policies; that is independent of the app.)

## Children
Worktime is a work tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights
Since we do not hold your data, requests for access, correction, portability and deletion are fulfilled directly in the app (view, edit, export, delete). If you are in the EEA/UK/Switzerland you also have the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

## Security
Data is kept in the app sandbox on your device. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode. Exported files are not encrypted; treat them like any other file containing your working hours.

## Changes
If the app ever changes what it collects, we will update this policy and the in-app Privacy screen **before** the change ships and, where required, ask for your consent first. The date above shows the latest revision.
